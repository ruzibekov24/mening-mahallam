Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes, completely free. Neither downloading nor using it costs anything.

Can others see my phone number?

Not unless you allow it. In settings you can turn off both showing your phone number to neighbours and displaying your last-seen time.

Why is it not on the Play Store?

Publishing to the Play Store takes a few days. Until then you download the app directly from this site. We will announce it once it is on the Play Store.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — the first release is for Android. An iOS version is planned.

My mahalla is not listed. What should I do?

Talk to your mahalla chairman and message the admin on Telegram (@Ilyos_Ruzibekov) — we will add your mahalla to the app.

How does signing up work?

You enter your phone number and confirm it through the Telegram bot — no waiting for an SMS. Then you scan your mahalla's QR code.

Who can see my request?

A private request is visible only to you and the mahalla staff. A public request is visible to everyone in the mahalla, and they can join it.

How much space does the app take?

The download is 52 MB. After installation it takes up roughly the same amount of space.
