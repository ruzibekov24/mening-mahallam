# Mahallam Vision

Version: v0.0.2

---

# Mission

To simplify communication, collaboration, and access to local information for every neighborhood (mahalla) in Uzbekistan.

---

# Vision

Mahallam aims to become the digital home of every mahalla in Uzbekistan.

Instead of relying on scattered Telegram groups, paper announcements, and offline communication, every resident should be able to access trusted local information, participate in community life, and communicate with their neighborhood through one simple mobile application.

---

# Problem

Today's neighborhood communication faces several challenges:

- Important announcements disappear in Telegram chats.
- Residents often miss events and important updates.
- Reporting local problems is inconvenient.
- Communication between residents and mahalla administration is fragmented.
- New residents struggle to integrate into the community.

---

# Solution

Mahallam provides one trusted platform where every neighborhood has its own digital space.

Residents can:

- Read official announcements
- Participate in community discussions
- Report local issues
- Join events
- Vote in polls
- Receive notifications
- Access neighborhood information

All within one mobile application.

---

# Target Users

Primary Users

- Residents
- Families
- Young people
- Elderly residents

Secondary Users

- Mahalla administration
- Volunteers
- Local organizations

---

# Core Principles

## Community First

Every feature should strengthen the local community.

## Simplicity

The application should be simple enough for anyone to use.

## Trust

Information should come from verified sources whenever possible.

## Accessibility

Everyone should be able to use Mahallam regardless of age or technical knowledge.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Mahallam aims to become the standard digital platform for neighborhood communication across Uzbekistan.

Future integrations may include public services, local businesses, community programs, and government digital services while maintaining a simple user experience.

---

# Success

Mahallam succeeds when residents open the application because it genuinely helps them stay informed, connected, and engaged with their neighborhood.
