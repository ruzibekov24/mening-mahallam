# Purpose

This document defines what Mahallam is NOT trying to become.

By clearly defining our non-goals, we avoid unnecessary complexity and stay focused on solving real neighborhood communication problems.

---

# Not Included in MVP

## ❌ Social Media Platform

Mahallam is not intended to replace Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, or other social media platforms.

Users come to Mahallam for neighborhood-related information, not entertainment.

---

## ❌ Private Messaging

One-to-one messaging will not be available in the first version.

Community interaction should happen through public posts, comments, and official announcements.

---

## ❌ Marketplace

Buying, selling, or advertising products is outside the scope of the MVP.

---

## ❌ Video Content Platform

Mahallam will not support short videos, reels, or creator-focused content.

---

## ❌ AI Features

Artificial intelligence is not part of the MVP.

AI will only be introduced if it solves a real user problem.

---

## ❌ Games & Gamification

Streaks, badges, leaderboards, and similar engagement mechanics are not planned for the initial release.

---

## ❌ Super App

Mahallam will focus on neighborhood communication only.

It is not intended to become an app for banking, shopping, food delivery, transportation, or unrelated services.

---

## ❌ Anonymous Accounts

Every account must belong to a verified resident of a specific mahalla.

Anonymous participation is not supported.

---

# Product Philosophy

Every feature must answer one question:

"Does this make neighborhood life easier?"

If the answer is no, the feature does not belong in Mahallam.
