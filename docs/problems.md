## Problem Statement

Neighborhood communication in Uzbekistan is currently fragmented across Telegram groups, paper announcements, and offline conversations.

While Telegram is widely used, it was never designed specifically for neighborhood management. Important announcements are often buried under spam, advertisements, and unrelated messages, making it difficult for residents to find reliable information.

Residents have no centralized place to follow local news, report issues, participate in community discussions, or stay informed about events happening in their own mahalla.

For mahalla administrators, managing communication through Telegram is equally challenging. Official announcements compete with everyday conversations, reducing visibility and engagement while making community management inefficient.

As a result, both residents and administrators spend more time searching for information than acting on it.

There is a clear need for a dedicated, modern platform built specifically for neighborhood communication—one that is organized, trusted, and easy for everyone to use.
