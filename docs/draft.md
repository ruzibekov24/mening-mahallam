# Non Goals

Version: v0.0.5

Status: Draft

---

## Purpose

To clearly define what Mahallam will NOT include in its first versions.

Avoiding unnecessary features allows us to build a simple, focused, and high-quality product.

---

## Not Included in MVP

### ❌ Entertainment Platform

Mahallam is not designed to replace Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube.

---

### ❌ Full Messaging Platform

Private one-to-one messaging will not be included in the MVP.

Residents communicate through posts, comments, and official channels.

---

### ❌ Marketplace

Buying and selling products is outside the scope of the first release.

---

### ❌ AI Features

Artificial intelligence will not be part of the MVP.

AI may be explored in future versions only if it solves a real problem.

---

### ❌ Games & Gamification

Badges, levels, streaks, and similar engagement systems are not planned for the initial release.

---

### ❌ Video-based Social Network

Mahallam is not intended to become a short-video or content-creator platform.

---

### ❌ Super App

Mahallam will focus only on neighborhood communication.

Other services may be considered in the future if they directly support this mission.

---

## Principle

Every new feature must solve a real neighborhood problem.

If a feature does not improve community communication or local life, it should not be added.
